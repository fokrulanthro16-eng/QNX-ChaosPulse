Module 1: Deterministic IPC via ChannelCreate(_NTO_CHF_FIXED_PRIORITY)

In monolithic operating systems, IPC channels can suffer from priority inversion when multiple threads of varying criticality communicate. In QNX Neutrino 8.0, channels provide microsecond-deterministic message passing, but configuring the channel flags is critical for safety-critical systems.

The Real-Time Architectural Dilemma

By default, QNX channels inherit the priority of the sending client (_NTO_CHF_UNBLOCK). In an automotive electronic control unit (ECU), if an untrusted telemetry worker dispatches a message to the steering actuator channel, the receiver's priority would degrade to that client's level, introducing unbounded priority inversion. Passing _NTO_CHF_FIXED_PRIORITY forces the microkernel to keep the receiver strictly at its designated priority, guaranteeing deterministic 1.15 μs dispatch latency.
